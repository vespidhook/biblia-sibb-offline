## Prompt do ícone

Use case: logo-brand. Create a production mobile app icon for Bíblia SIBB, a Portuguese Bible reader. Square 1024x1024 PNG, full bleed opaque solid deep blue #1e3a8a background, no rounded outer corners. Center a beautifully balanced, bold ivory-white open Bible with a small cross rising from its central spine, tasteful restrained light blue page accent #60a5fa. Flat elegant geometric silhouette, crisp clean edges, premium editorial simplicity, recognizable at 32px. Entire symbol occupies central 58% width and 54% height, generous clear margins. No text, no letters, no mockup, no frame, no shadows, no gradients, no watermark. Deliver the icon alone.

## Prompt da splash

Use case: logo-brand. Generate a square native mobile splash screen logo asset, 1024x1024. Completely opaque perfectly uniform solid background RGB(30,58,138), hex #1e3a8a. In the very center place a SMALL white open Bible with a white Christian cross rising from the middle and two light blue #60a5fa curved page accents. Elegant clean flat geometric shapes, flowing symmetric pages, rounded cross ends. The WHOLE symbol MUST fit inside a 500x500 central region, with at least 260px blank blue margins on ALL FOUR sides. No transparency, no noise, no texture, no gradients, no shadows, no text, no border, no mockup. The solid background should reach all edges. Carefully drawn smooth edges.

## Validação visual

Conferir a splash e os recortes do launcher em um build de produção Android/iOS. Expo Go não reproduz integralmente a splash nativa.
