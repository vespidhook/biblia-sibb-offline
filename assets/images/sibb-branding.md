## Prompt do ícone

Use case: identity-preserve. Edit the provided church logo into a square mobile app icon. Input image is the official SIBB logo and must retain the EXACT hand-lettered lowercase sibb fish outline, shape of all letters, dot, curved fish body and crossed tail. Extract only the top fish emblem, omit the church name below. Place that exact black emblem centered both vertically and horizontally on a pure white opaque square canvas. Emblem total width 60% of canvas, original aspect ratio maintained, generous white margins. Output 1024x1024 PNG. Strictly preserve original identity; do not redesign, do not add a Bible, cross or other elements, no shadows, gradients, textures, frame or rounded outer corners. Crisp black original logo on white.
